Data and preregistered feasibility rules

The final confirmatory analysis used 2024-05 and 2024-11. The frozen Protocol v2 feasibility process excluded 2024-02 and 2024-08 under the registered 64-node / 98% training-coverage rule. January 2025 was excluded from all confirmatory inference because it had been used in exploratory development.

The inferential unit was the target calendar day after node-level and forecast-origin aggregation and seed averaging, yielding 10 paired confirmatory days in the registered hypothesis tests.

Uncertainty and multiplicity

Effect uncertainty was estimated with a preregistered 10,000-replicate hierarchical bootstrap resampling confirmatory month and then target day within month, with fixed RNG seed 20260814. H2, H3 and the three H4 horizon-specific tests formed one secondary family and were adjusted using the Holm procedure.

Research interpretation

The confirmatory evidence does not support a claim that the registered directed-road Dynamic Graph Transformer broadly outperforms the Temporal MLP. The scientifically relevant outcome is the transparent registered result itself: small and uncertain +1h benefit, non-significant primary inference, and a secondary short-vs-long horizon pattern that did not survive the preregistered multiplicity correction.
