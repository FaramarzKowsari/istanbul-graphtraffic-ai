Open-source graph neural network research for Istanbul traffic prediction

İstanbul GraphTraffic AI is a reproducible research project for Istanbul traffic forecasting using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks (ST-GNNs) and a Dynamic Graph Transformer. The project models traffic sensors and road relationships as graphs and studies +1h, +2h, +3h and +6h forecasting, predictive uncertainty, directed road topology and robustness when traffic sensors become unavailable.

Research questions

Road topology

Does a directed road-topology graph improve traffic prediction compared with purely geographic sensor adjacency?

Adaptive graph learning

Can learned graph connectivity capture traffic dependencies that a static physical network misses?

Multi-horizon prediction

How do ST-GNN and Graph Transformer models behave at +1h, +2h, +3h and +6h horizons?

Predictive uncertainty

How well calibrated are prediction intervals across congestion regimes and time periods?

Sensor-failure resilience

How does forecasting performance degrade when 10%, 20% or 30% of sensors become unavailable?

Reproducible evidence

Research claims are tied to machine-readable experiment outputs rather than manually inserted benchmark numbers.
