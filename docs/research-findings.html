Istanbul Traffic Forecasting Research Evaluation

This page documents how İstanbul GraphTraffic AI will evaluate graph-based traffic forecasting without overstating results. No final real-data superiority claim is frozen until confirmatory experiments produce machine-readable metrics artifacts.

Current status: v0.1.0 is a research protocol and executable pipeline. Real-data benchmark findings are not yet frozen.

Planned evidence

Forecast horizons

Separate +1h, +2h, +3h and +6h evaluation.

Point metrics

MAE, RMSE, MAPE and R² by horizon and slice.

Graph ablations

Geographic kNN, directed road topology and learned adaptive graph comparisons.

Predictive uncertainty

Quantile coverage and prediction interval width.

Sensor failures

Random and structured 10%, 20% and 30% sensor outages.

Confirmatory statistics

Paired statistical comparisons before superiority claims.

Models under evaluation

Persistence, Historical Average, Temporal MLP, GRU-based temporal forecasting, ST-GCN-style graph forecasting and Dynamic Graph Transformer models are included in the research pipeline.
